Nelson

Nelson is a three year old goat who loves a good scratch in his beard.

Gus

The loudest baby pig you will ever meet, when he isn't busy stuffing his face with whatever he can get into.

The Beatles

The four most rock and roll horses you will ever meet. John, Paul, George, and Ringo were all rescued from a racetrack that was set to put them down. Now they spend their days running for fun!

Dolores & Dot

Our proud mother Dolores with the first rabbit born on our barn, Dot. The two are inseparable and can often be found snuggling in some hay together

The Golden Girls

Blanche, Dorothy, Sophia, and Rose are the most relaxed hens you will ever meet. We recently adopted a new hen, Cynthia, who quickly fell in with the group. They love nothing more than standing around and bawking to eachother.

Laura

The fluffiest animal that has ever set foot in our barn. Laura is very affectionate and loves nothing more than being picked up and held.
