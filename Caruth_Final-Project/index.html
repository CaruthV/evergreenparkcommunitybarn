Where Nature and Nurture meets Education

Here at the Evergreen Park Community Barn we have one mission, and it's simple: Using our family to help you connect with yours. Our family just happens to consist of people as well as farm animals, but we all love eachother just the same. When you and your family come to the barn, there are no shortages of activities to enjoy. You can enjoy a simple picnic in our field, look at (and even hold) some of our animals, learn a little something about animals from one of our staff, or attend one of our many events that we host throughout the year. We're even available to host weddings! No matter what your reason for visiting, we guarantee your time here will be meaningful and remembered for a life time.

We can't wait to see you!
