Frank Williams

Meet the man that started it all! Frank is the original founder of the Evergreen Park Community Barn. He grew up as the youngest son to a family of ranchers from Texas. When he moved to Evergreen Park he decided to bring some of the farm down with him! He opened the barn back in 2015 and never looked back, providing education and relaxation to anyone who comes to visit.

Ellen Williams

Oldest daughter of Frank and best rider the barn has ever seen. She's helped her father with the barn since she was a teen and returned after college to help him run things better than ever. When she isn't attending the animals directly she spearheads the events department of the barn.

Rick Davis

Newest addition to the human staff members here at Evergreen Park Community Barn and proud husband of Ellen. Having never set foot on a farm before, he was hesitant to come help out initially. Seeing Ellen's passion for animals and the family's committment to the community, he couldn't help but roll up his sleeves and get out there.
