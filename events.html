We Love Having You Here!

Here at the Evergreen Park Community Barn, we do our best to host events all year round. In the warmer months, we host anything from picnics in the grass to dinners inside the barn. In the fall we offer hay rides and trick or treating for the little guests. Check this page frequently to see what we have cooking up and get yourself down here!

If you're interested in organizing a private event in our space, feel free to head over to our contact page and we will get back to you as soon as possible.
